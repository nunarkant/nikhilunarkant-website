You get a clear, prioritized picture of where to focus next and a concrete plan for turning AI ambition into measurable impact.

Det de fleste spørger omWhat most people ask

Hvem er Nikhil Unarkant?

Nikhil Unarkant er AI-ledelsesrådgiver og forfatter til bogen AI Leadership. Han har over 10 års erfaring fra Novo Nordisk, bl.a. som AI-projektleder og leder af tech- og innovationsteams, og hjælper ledere med at gøre AI til værdi i store organisationer.

Hvad koster et seminar?

Seminaret Teknisk koster DKK 18.950. Seminarerne Strategisk, Organisatorisk og Ledelse koster DKK 22.250 hver.

Hvor lang tid tager rådgivningsforløbene?

En gæstetale varer 1–2 timer. En AI-audit tager 1–2 måneder, opkvalificering af medarbejdere 2–4 måneder, og AI-champions-forløbet 4–6 måneder.

Kan forløbene tilpasses vores organisation?

Ja. Seminarerne kan tages hver for sig eller kombineres til et samlet forløb, og rådgivningen tilpasses organisationens udgangspunkt. Kontakt mig for en første snak.

Hvad er forskellen på et seminar og et rådgivningsforløb?

Et seminar er en fokuseret endagsworkshop. Rådgivningsforløbene er længere og går i dybden med organisationen, fx en AI-audit eller uddannelse af AI-champions.

Who is Nikhil Unarkant?

Nikhil Unarkant is an AI leadership advisor and the author of the book AI Leadership. He has more than 10 years of experience from Novo Nordisk, including as an AI project manager and leader of tech and innovation teams, and helps leaders turn AI into value in large organizations.

What does a seminar cost?

The Technical seminar costs DKK 18,950. The Strategic, Organizational and Leadership seminars cost DKK 22,250 each.

How long do the advisory engagements take?

A guest speech lasts 1–2 hours. An AI audit takes 1–2 months, employee upskilling 2–4 months, and the AI champions programme 4–6 months.

Can the engagements be tailored to our organization?

Yes. The seminars can be taken individually or combined into one programme, and the advisory is tailored to the organization's starting point. Get in touch for a first conversation.

What is the difference between a seminar and an advisory engagement?

A seminar is a focused one-day workshop. Advisory engagements are longer and go deeper into the organization, for example an AI audit or training AI champions.

Skal vi finde det rette forløb til jer? Shall we find the right programme for you?

Seminarerne kan tages hver for sig eller sættes sammen til et samlet forløb, der er tilpasset jeres organisation og udgangspunkt. Lad os tage en snak om, hvad der passer til jer.

The seminars can be taken individually or combined into a full programme tailored to your organization and starting point. Let's talk about what fits you.
